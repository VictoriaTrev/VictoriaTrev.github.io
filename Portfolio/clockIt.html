Clock It!

What is Clock It?

Clock it is a semester long project that was completed by a small team of 3 students at NMSU who were: Elizabeth Torres, Renae Hunt, and Victoria Treviño. The program allows student organizations such as clubs to log hours and be more organized with their tasks and teams. The program allows the user to access a calendar, a clock, and an overview page.

Our main functions were to let the user be able to clock in and out, access the calendar, and list tasks. One of the main goals was to create a functioning app that made it easy to get to different places faster without needing to go through multiple pages. During the creation of our app, we also performed research throughout the process and kept in touch with our participants to allow us to get feedback to better our program.

Research Overview

We conducted two user studies using surveys and interviews with different participants gathered by each team member. These studies were performed at different points during the semester. The team also maintained ongoing contact with participants to ensure our application met user expectations and improved upon existing solutions.

User Study 1

This was our initial study where participants reviewed our low-fidelity prototype. We presented sketches and Figma designs to gather feedback on the overall concept, color scheme, and visual layout.

  • General thoughts about the application
  • Color scheme
  • Visual design of objects and items
User Study 2

This high-fidelity prototype study focused on functionality. Participants interacted with Google Slides that simulated app behavior, helping us identify improvements that would make the app more intuitive and efficient.
