CSCI 4255 Digital Game Design (DGD)

Description:

Introductory tutorial from the Godot website to learn the basics about the platform. Once the class had gone through the tutorial, our assignment was to change it either functionally or visually. I had created an alterations to be an underwater scene and allowed the player to pick up "speed boost bubbles".

To play the game yourself click: DGD "Dodge The Creeps Alterations"

CSCI 4255 Digital Game Design (DGD)

Description:

Game Jam 1 of the Fall 2025 semester worked with a group of 3 students. We made a game with the theme being "I'm Lost!". We had to create a platformer game over the course of 2 weeks then playtest all of the groups. Out game is based off the player being an ant with the main goal of finding the "boat" to end the game by sailing off into the water.

To play the game yourself click: DGD "ANTventure"

CSCI 4255 Digital Game Design (DGD)

Description:

Game Jam 2 of the Fall 2025 semester worked with a group of 3 students. We made a game with the theme being "Delivery", but it had to be multiplayer. With two weeks again, we made a vehicle racing game to pick up the most amount of food (points) before the time runs out.

To play the game yourself click: DGD "Midnight Munchies"

CSCI 4255 Digital Game Design (DGD)

Description:

Game Jam 4 of the Fall 2025 semester worked with a group of 5 students. We made a game with the theme being "Swarm". We made an undercover bear (Justin Beebear) in a hive of bees game. The player is a disguised bear trying to steal honey in time for hibernation. Players have to fly under the radar and not set off the "sus" meter or else they will get discovered by the queen bee and, becoming eliminated.

To play the game yourself click: DGD "Angry Bees"
