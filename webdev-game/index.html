Multiplayer Game - Mancala

Rules:

    Game moves counter-clockwise
    Once you click the seeds in a pit, you must use that set
    Seeds can be placed in any pit and your store
    You cannot place seeds in opponents store

Special Rules:

    If your last seed ends in your store, you gain an extra turn :O

How to Play:

    When you are ready to play, click the "Play" button
    To restart the game simply click the "Restart Game" button
    Player 1 is pink/red - if you are player 1 you can hover over your colored pits once you click it, you are locked in 'v'
    Player 2 is cyan/blue - if you are player 2 you can hover over your colored pits once you click it, you are locked in :p
    You continue to play back and forth until one player wins
    Bonus turn: the player that gets the bonus gets to pick another pit

End of Game:

    The game ends when either player has no more seeds in their pit lot
    Whoever has the most seeds in their store wins!!! :D
    TO CLOSE THE RULES, CLICK THE "Rule" BUTTON AGAIN
    I hope you enjoy the game :D
